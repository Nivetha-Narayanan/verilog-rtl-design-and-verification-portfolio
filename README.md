## RTL Design and Verification
Welcome to my hardware engineering repository. This space serves as a professional log of my journey into RTL (Register Transfer Level) Design and Verification.
​Rather than just writing code, I use this repository to document the "Hardware Mindset"—the transition from sequential software thinking to the concurrent, timing-driven world of Digital VLSI.
